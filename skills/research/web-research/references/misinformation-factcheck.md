# Fact-Check Pattern: ATM Inventor (Vietnamese Misinformation)

## The Myth
"Người Việt Nam Đỗ Đức Cường phát minh ra máy ATM" — widely circulated in Vietnamese media.

## The Facts (Wikipedia-verified)

| Person | Role | Year | Wikipedia |
|--------|------|------|-----------|
| **John Shepherd-Barron** | First ATM machine (Barclays, Enfield) | June 1967 | John_Shepherd-Barron |
| **James Goodfellow** | PIN + plastic card (modern ATM standard) | 1966 patent | James_Goodfellow |
| **Đỗ Đức Cường** | Citibank senior engineer, improved ATM systems | 1977+ | Not on Wikipedia |

## Why This Happens
- Đỗ Đức Cường was a prolific Vietnamese-American inventor (50+ patents) at Citibank
- He made significant improvements to ATM software/hardware systems
- Vietnamese media inflated "senior engineer at Citibank who improved ATM" to "inventor of ATM"
- The actual ATM was invented 10 years before he joined Citibank

## Verification Method
1. Search English Wikipedia: `who invented ATM`
2. Google Scholar / BBC / Guardian for primary sources
3. If Vietnamese source claims something that conflicts with international consensus, **cross-check with English-language authoritative sources**
4. Wikipedia REST API is the fastest verification tool:
   ```bash
   ddgs text -q "John Shepherd-Barron ATM inventor" -m 3
   ddgs text -q "James Goodfellow PIN ATM" -m 3
   ```

## Signal Phrases (when to fact-check Vietnamese claims)
- "Người Việt phát minh ra..." (a Vietnamese person invented...)
- "Cha đẻ của..." (father of...)
- Claims that sound too good to be true about a Vietnamese figure
- Especially common for: ATM, search engines, social media platforms
